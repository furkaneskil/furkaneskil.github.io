Computer Engineer

I'm Furkan Eskil, a Computer Engineer from Turkey and a Cukurova University graduate. I care about building from fundamentals: programming languages, data structures, algorithms, databases, Linux, Git, request/response flows, WebSocket communication and socket programming.

Professionally, I have worked on inventory management flows and RESTful API endpoints at Ploud, OCPP 1.6/WebSocket communication for EV charging systems at Maksopus, and earlier backend foundations around MVC architecture and ORM usage at Provera. Those experiences shaped how I think about well-defined APIs, explicit data flow, predictable state and boring reliability.

I like learning close to the metal of the problem: reading documentation, tracing how data moves, testing assumptions, and breaking things down until the system feels explainable. Outside of coding, I write and think about software architecture, engineering tradeoffs, and the parts of real-world development that do not fit neatly into tutorials.

The part I enjoy most is turning uncertainty into structure: finding the shape of a problem, designing the path forward, and building the thing.
